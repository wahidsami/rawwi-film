# Semantic Signals

Semantic signals are non-legal indicators used to interpret meaning.

They do not establish violation.
They do not create findings.

## 1. Speaker signals

- pronouns
- vocatives
- quotation marks
- dialogue tags
- attribution phrases
- scene structure

## 2. Listener signals

- second-person address
- imperative verbs
- direct vocatives
- response markers
- reply chains

## 3. Target signals

- noun references
- named entities
- group references
- relational language
- descriptive pronouns

## 4. Narrative signals

- dialogue formatting
- narrator framing
- screenplay formatting
- scene headings
- documentary language
- news language
- testimony language

## 5. Intent signals

- sentiment
- negation
- approval words
- condemnation words
- laughter markers
- irony markers
- warning markers
- instructional markers

## 6. Context signals

- Story Memory continuity
- Scene Memory continuity
- local neighboring sentences
- later reversal
- earlier setup

## 7. Risk signals

- explicit threat language
- mocking language
- quoted harmful language
- educational framing
- documentary framing
- fictional framing

## 8. Ambiguity signals

- unclear speaker
- unclear target
- unclear quotation boundary
- mixed tone
- conflicting context
