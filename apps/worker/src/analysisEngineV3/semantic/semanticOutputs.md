# Semantic Outputs

The semantic layer emits meaning objects, not legal findings.

## Required outputs

- Semantic Meaning
- Narrative Intent
- Conversation Role
- Scene Role
- Target
- Victim
- Speaker
- Listener
- Emotion
- Risk Context
- Confidence

## Optional outputs

- semantic notes
- ambiguity notes
- context reversal notes
- quotation boundary notes
- speaker ambiguity notes

## Output rules

- Outputs must remain non-legal.
- Outputs may explain meaning.
- Outputs may not classify legality.
- Outputs may not construct findings.
- Outputs may not decide whether to report.
